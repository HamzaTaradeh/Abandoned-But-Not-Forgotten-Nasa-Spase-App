ABOUT THE PROJECT

Remembering the explorers.

Abandoned But Not Forgotten is a story-driven educational website about human-made machines left on the Moon, Mars and beyond.

Our mission

To make space exploration approachable through the stories of machines that continue to teach us, even after their missions have ended.

How we count the catalog

The catalog has 17 story entries. Some entries group Viking 1 and 2; Spirit and Opportunity; Phoenix and InSight; Pioneer 10 and 11; and the three Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicles. Therefore, 17 is the number of catalog records, not a strict count of individual hardware objects.

Research & references

Use primary NASA sources when verifying facts. Suggested starting points: NASA Science, NASA, NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive and JPL Photojournal.

This is an educational student project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA. Each artifact detail page links to NASA/JPL/NIST references. Verify each claim against the linked primary sources before submission.
